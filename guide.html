Model results

Isolation Forest · 4 features. Higher contamination: more recall, less precision.

Amount vs usual

ln(USD ÷ merchant median)

Card country mismatch

1 if card ≠ shopper country

30-min velocity

Shopper's payments in the last 30 min

Declines today

Shopper's declines that day

Share flaggedFlaggedPrecisionRecall
0.5%1,6540.980.50
1% (pipeline default)3,3390.670.69
2%6,6720.350.73

What this shows: precision (share of flags that are planted anomalies) and recall (share of planted anomalies that get flagged) at three contamination rates.

Source: REACH_DS_DEV.PAYMENTS_ANOMALY.PAYMENT_FEATURES, 334,850 payments from the last 30 days, 3,222 of them planted.

How it was derived: scikit-learn IsolationForest (200 trees, random_state=42), fit on a 200k sample and scored on all rows, using the same code as the notebook's experiment cell. Run on 2026-11-23.

Caveat: synthetic data, so the numbers shift slightly if the data is regenerated. Real data has no planted flag to grade against.

Discussion: cost of a false alarm vs a missed fraud sets the threshold.
